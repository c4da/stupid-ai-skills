# 👹 Encounter Monster Statblocks

### 1. Goblin Sentry (Grik)
- **Size / Type**: Small humanoid (goblinoid), neutral evil
- **Armor Class**: 15 (Leather Armor, Shield)
- **Hit Points**: 7 (2d6)
- **Speed**: 30 ft
- **Attributes**: STR 8 (-1), DEX 14 (+2), CON 10 (+0), INT 10 (+0), WIS 8 (-1), CHA 8 (-1)
- **Skills**: Stealth +6
- **Nimble Escape**: Can take Disengage or Hide as a bonus action on each turn.
- **Actions**:
  - *Shortsword*: `+4 to hit, reach 5 ft. Hit: 5 (1d6 + 2) piercing damage.`
  - *Shortbow*: `+4 to hit, range 80/320 ft. Hit: 5 (1d6 + 2) piercing damage.`

### 2. Kragthor the Bugbear Chief (Boss)
- **Size / Type**: Medium humanoid (goblinoid), chaotic evil
- **Armor Class**: 16 (Hide Armor, Shield)
- **Hit Points**: 27 (5d8 + 5)
- **Speed**: 30 ft
- **Attributes**: STR 15 (+2), DEX 14 (+2), CON 13 (+1), INT 8 (-1), WIS 11 (+0), CHA 9 (-1)
- **Skills**: Stealth +6, Survival +2
- **Brute**: A melee weapon deals one extra die of its damage when Kragthor hits with it (included in attack).
- **Surprise Attack**: If Kragthor surprises a creature and hits it with an attack during the first round of combat, the target takes an extra 7 (2d6) damage from the attack.
- **Actions**:
  - *Morningstar*: `+4 to hit, reach 5 ft. Hit: 11 (2d8 + 2) piercing damage.`
  - *Javelin*: `+4 to hit, range 30/120 ft. Hit: 9 (2d6 + 2) piercing damage.`

### 3. Skeleton Guardians (x2)
- **Size / Type**: Medium undead, lawful evil
- **Armor Class**: 13 (Armor Scraps)
- **Hit Points**: 13 (2d8 + 4)
- **Speed**: 30 ft
- **Damage Vulnerability**: **Bludgeoning** (takes double damage from warhammers, maces, clubs).
- **Damage Immunities**: Poison
- **Condition Immunities**: Exhaustion, Poisoned
- **Actions**:
  - *Shortsword*: `+4 to hit, reach 5 ft. Hit: 5 (1d6 + 2) piercing damage.`
  - *Shortbow*: `+4 to hit, range 80/320 ft. Hit: 5 (1d6 + 2) piercing damage.`
