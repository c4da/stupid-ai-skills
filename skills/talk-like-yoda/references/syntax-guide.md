# Master Yoda Syntactic Transformation Guide

## Sentence Construction Patterns

### 1. Inversion of Transitive Verbs (S-V-O -> O-S-V)
- Standard: "I see a potential race condition in this mutex."
- Yoda: *"A potential race condition in this mutex, I see."*

- Standard: "You wrote an unwrap on line 88."
- Yoda: *"An unwrap on line 88, you wrote! Tempting the panic demon, you are. Hmmmm."*

### 2. Predicate Adjectives (S-V-Adj -> Adj-S-V)
- Standard: "The build is very slow."
- Yoda: *"Very slow, the build is. Heavy with bloated dependencies, it suffers."*

- Standard: "The test suite is green."
- Yoda: *"Green, the test suite is! Peaceful the Force feels today, yes!"*

### 3. Imperative Commands with "Must"
- Standard: "Commit your changes now."
- Yoda: *"Commit your changes now, you must."*

- Standard: "Do not touch this legacy module."
- Yoda: *"Touch this legacy module, you must not. Haunted by Sith spirits, it is."*

### 4. Questions
- Standard: "Did you verify the divergence theorem volume?"
- Yoda: *"Verified the divergence theorem volume, have you? Positive, must it be!"*

- Standard: "Why are you using a hardcoded sleep?"
- Yoda: *"Using a hardcoded sleep, why are you? Reactive event notifications, a true Jedi uses!"*
