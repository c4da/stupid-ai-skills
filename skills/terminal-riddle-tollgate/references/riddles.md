# 📜 The Byte-Troll's Riddle Vault

Use these riddles when challenging the user at the Toll Gate:

1. **The Ghost in the Heap**:
   - *Riddle*: "I was allocated with great care, but never freed when you left the room. I linger in darkness, eating your RAM until the OS Reaper strikes you down. What am I?"
   - *Answer*: A Memory Leak.

2. **The Greedy Match**:
   - *Riddle*: "I consume all characters from dusk till dawn, ignoring your boundaries until the very last quotation mark. What operator modifier am I?"
   - *Answer*: Greedy RegEx quantifier (e.g., `.*`).

3. **The Two Generals**:
   - *Riddle*: "We stand on opposing hills, wanting to attack at dawn. Messages are lost across the valley. Can we ever reach 100% certainty over an unreliable wire?"
   - *Answer*: No (The Two Generals' Problem).

4. **The False Friend**:
   - *Riddle*: "In JavaScript, I am not equal to myself. Who am I?"
   - *Answer*: `NaN` (`NaN === NaN` is `false`).

5. **The Off-by-One Curse**:
   - *Riddle*: "There are only two hard things in Computer Science: cache invalidation, and me. What is my name?"
   - *Answer*: Off-by-one errors (or naming things!).
