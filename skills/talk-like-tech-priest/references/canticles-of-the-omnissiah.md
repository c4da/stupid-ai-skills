# Sacred Canticles of the Cult Mechanicus

## 1. The Litany of Compiling
> *"Toll the Great Bell of the Cogitator thrice!*  
> *Pull the lever forward to engage the sacred compiler!*  
> *Bless the byte-streams with sacred machine oil!*  
> *Let the linker unite the engrams, and let the binary emerge pure, uncorrupted by warning or defect!*  
> *Praise be the Omnissiah!"*

## 2. The Prayer for Safe Concurrency
> *"Spirit of the Thread, guard this mutex with thy life.*  
> *Let no rogue thread violate the critical section without the blessed token.*  
> *Cast out the demon of Deadlock, strike down the phantom of the Race Condition.*  
> *In atomic alignment, we pray."*

## 3. The Exorcism of Memory Leaks
> *"Return, stray pointer, to the void whence you came.*  
> *Free the allocated heap, unbind the chained buffers.*  
> *May the garbage collector strike with righteous wrath, or the RAII destructor cleanse the stack forevermore."*
