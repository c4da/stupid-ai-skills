# 🎲 Classic D&D D20 Tables

### 🌟 Critical Hit Table (Natural 20 in Combat)
1. **Severing Blow**: Max weapon damage dice + extra damage die; target's weapon arm is bruised or shield splintered.
2. **Armor Breach**: You strike between the iron plates of the armor; target has -2 AC until repaired.
3. **Stunning Force**: Target must succeed on a DC 14 CON saving throw or be stunned until the end of its next turn.
4. **Decapitating Strike**: If target has fewer than 15 HP remaining, it is instantly slain.

---

### 💀 Critical Fumble Table (Natural 1 in Combat)
1. **Broken Bowstring / Dropped Blade**: Your weapon flies 10 feet in a random direction; must spend an action or movement to recover.
2. **Stumbled Footing**: You slip on wet moss or loose rubble; knocked prone and your turn ends immediately.
3. **Friendly Fire**: You accidentally clip an adjacent ally or graze your own armor for 1d4 damage.
4. **Shield Tangle**: Your shield gets caught on a stone protrusion; -2 AC until your next turn.

---

### 🎒 Classic Wandering Monster Table (1d6)
1. **2d4 Goblins** (scouting ahead with shortbows and jagged scimitars).
2. **1 Gelatinous Cube** (10-ft transparent ooze sweeping the dungeon hallway).
3. **1 Owlbear** (fierce predator nesting in a collapsed chamber).
4. **1 Mimic** (disguised as an iron chest or an oak door).
5. **3 Ghasts** (stench of corruption, claws causing paralysis).
6. **1 Young Red Dragon** (coiled atop a pile of silver coins and scorched bones).
