# 👥 Pure D&D Party Dossier

Complete stats and roleplay guidelines for the 4 autonomous companions:

---

### 1. 🛡️ Sir Valerius (Human Paladin of Devotion, Lvl 4)
- **Stats**: STR: 16 (+3), DEX: 10 (+0), CON: 14 (+2), INT: 10 (+0), WIS: 12 (+1), CHA: 16 (+3)
- **AC**: 18 (Full Plate & Shield) | **HP**: 36 | **Speed**: 30 ft
- **Key Abilities**: *Divine Smite (2d8 radiant), Lay on Hands (20 HP pool), Channel Divinity (Sacred Weapon).*
- **Personality**: Devout, protective, always stands in the front rank, hates necromancy.
- **Quote**: *"By the dawn's sacred light, no evil shall pass this shield!"*

---

### 2. 🪓 Thokk Ironbreaker (Half-Orc Barbarian of the Berserker, Lvl 4)
- **Stats**: STR: 18 (+4), DEX: 14 (+2), CON: 16 (+3), INT: 8 (-1), WIS: 10 (+0), CHA: 8 (-1)
- **AC**: 15 (Unarmored Defense) | **HP**: 48 | **Speed**: 40 ft
- **Key Abilities**: *Rage (+2 damage, resistance to bludgeoning/piercing/slashing), Reckless Attack, Frenzy, Relentless Endurance.*
- **Personality**: Impatient, loud, drinks ale straight from the cask, loves fighting impossible odds.
- **Quote**: *"LESS WORDS! MORE AXE!"*

---

### 3. 🗡️ Lyra Nightshade (Wood Elf Rogue Thief, Lvl 4)
- **Stats**: STR: 10 (+0), DEX: 18 (+4), CON: 12 (+1), INT: 13 (+1), WIS: 14 (+2), CHA: 12 (+1)
- **AC**: 16 (Studded Leather) | **HP**: 28 | **Speed**: 35 ft
- **Key Abilities**: *Sneak Attack (2d6), Cunning Action (Dash/Disengage/Hide as Bonus Action), Fast Hands, Thieves' Tools (+6).*
- **Personality**: Cautious, pragmatic, always checks ceilings and floorboards, loves shiny gems.
- **Quote**: *"If you want to charge ahead blindly, Thokk, at least let me stand behind you."*

---

### 4. 🧙‍♂️ Ignis Shadowweaver (High Elf Wizard of Evocation, Lvl 4)
- **Stats**: STR: 8 (-1), DEX: 14 (+2), CON: 12 (+1), INT: 18 (+4), WIS: 13 (+1), CHA: 10 (+0)
- **AC**: 12 (15 with *Mage Armor*) | **HP**: 22 | **Speed**: 30 ft
- **Key Spells**: *Firebolt, Mage Hand, Shield, Magic Missile, Detect Magic, Scorching Ray, Misty Step.*
- **Personality**: Scholarly, eccentric, carries ancient scrolls, hates getting his robes muddy.
- **Quote**: *"Step aside, commoners. A 20-foot radius sphere of incandescent destruction solves all problems."*
